Mechanics

  • Smart Image Analysis: The system uses a trained "digital eye" to look at photos of skin spots and lesions.
  • Pattern Recognition: It compares your photo against a library of thousands of medical images to identify specific shapes, colors, and textures.
  • Risk Categorization: It automatically sorts findings into two groups: Benign (low risk/harmless) or Malignant (high risk/requires medical attention).
  • High-Speed Results: The AI processes the complex visual data in seconds to give an instant assessment based on its training.

Limitations

  • Not a Doctor: This tool provides an automated assessment and is not a formal medical diagnosis.
  • Image Quality: The accuracy depends heavily on the photo; blurry or poorly lit images may lead to incorrect results.
  • Limited Scope: The AI is specifically trained on a set of seven common skin conditions and may not recognize rarer issues.
  • Data Balance: Because there are naturally more "harmless" cases in the world, the AI must use weighted math to ensure it doesn't overlook "dangerous" cases.
  • Professional Follow-up: Any "Malignant" result should be immediately verified by a dermatologist for a physical examination.
